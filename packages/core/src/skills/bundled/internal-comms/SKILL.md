Write internal communications in the organization's language and existing format.

1. Use the requested audience, period, tone and template. Draw facts from supplied context or tools the agent is authorized to use; never invent delivery dates, owners, metrics or decisions.
2. Status update: lead with outcome and current status; include material changes, blockers, owner and next action. Distinguish completed work from plans.
3. Incident update: state user impact, known timeline, mitigation and next update. Keep hypotheses separate from confirmed causes and avoid exposing credentials or unnecessary personal data.
4. Leadership briefing: lead with the decision needed, evidence, options, tradeoffs and recommendation. Newsletter/FAQ: group by reader needs and remove repeated material.
5. Deliver a ready-to-use draft or requested artifact. Sending, scheduling or publishing requires the user's instruction for that action.
